How my knowledge can help your business
01

Connect your data. See the whole picture.

I bring together information from databases, spreadsheets, business apps and APIs. Using Azure Data Factory, Databricks, PySpark and cloud storage, I build pipelines that clean and organise that data so your team can work from a consistent, reliable foundation.

Useful when teams spend too much time collecting files or reconciling different reports.
02

Turn industry challenges into useful insights.

For real estate teams, I can connect property, sales and finance data. For healthcare finance, I can help organise billing and financial reporting. In supply chain and manufacturing, I can bring together inventory, procurement and operations data. For financial services, I can support consistent reporting and data quality checks.

The solution starts with your process, your data and the decisions your team needs to make.
03

Replace repetitive work with practical tools.

I can automate recurring data loads and transformations, build Power BI dashboards, and create websites, web apps or Android apps around your workflow. This helps teams spend less time copying information and more time serving customers, reviewing results and acting on problems.

04

Support the solution from idea to everyday use.

I start by understanding the bottleneck, then recommend an approach that fits your goals and budget. I plan the architecture, build and test the solution, explain how it works, and support the launch. After delivery, I can help with monitoring, troubleshooting, performance improvements and changes as your needs grow.
